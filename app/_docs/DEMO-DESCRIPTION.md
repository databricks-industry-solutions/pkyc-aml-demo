# Databricks Lakehouse for Ongoing KYC/AML: Executive Demo

## Overview

This executive demo showcases how Databricks Lakehouse Platform transforms financial crime prevention by unifying KYC (Know Your Customer), AML (Anti-Money Laundering), and compliance operations across all three lines of defense. The demo demonstrates a complete end-to-end value chain from customer onboarding through transaction monitoring, investigation, regulatory reporting, and executive oversight—all powered by AI, real-time analytics, and unified data governance.

## Business Value Proposition

### Three Pillars of Value

1. **Enhanced KYC without Friction**
   - Reduce customer onboarding time from days to seconds
   - Improve customer experience and Net Promoter Score (NPS)
   - Enable real-time risk assessment and instant verification

2. **Unified Financial Crime Prevention**
   - Single platform for financial crime prevention across all lines of defense
   - Leverage AI, ML, and data to detect and prevent financial crime
   - Reduce false positives and improve detection accuracy

3. **Unified Data & Governance**
   - Complete audit trail of all data processing and access
   - PII protection and centralized governance
   - Federated administration capabilities for local jurisdictions

## Target Audience

**Primary:** CRO (Chief Risk Officer), MLRO (Money Laundering Reporting Officer), COO (Chief Operating Officer), CCO (Chief Compliance Officer)

**Secondary:** Head of Financial Crime, Head of Compliance, Head of Risk Management, Data & Analytics Leaders

## Demo Flow (6 Scenes)

### Scene 1: Seamless KYC Onboarding
**Duration:** 3-4 minutes  
**Key Technologies:** Agent Bricks, Mosaic AI, Unity Catalog  
**Value Story:** Demonstrate AI-powered customer onboarding with real-time verification and instant risk assessment. Show how Agent Bricks automates document processing, identity verification, and risk scoring—reducing onboarding time from days to seconds while maintaining compliance.

**Key Talking Points:**
- Real-time document extraction and verification
- Automated risk scoring using ML models
- Instant decision-making with complete audit trail
- Integration with external data sources for PEP/sanctions screening

---

### Scene 2: Real-Time Transaction Screening
**Duration:** 3-4 minutes  
**Key Technologies:** Databricks Streaming, Delta Lake, ML Models  
**Value Story:** Show live streaming analytics dashboard displaying SWIFT messages screened in real-time with ML-based alerting. Demonstrate how streaming analytics enables immediate detection of suspicious transactions before they complete.

**Key Talking Points:**
- Real-time transaction processing at scale
- ML-based alert generation with explainability
- Low-latency decision-making for transaction blocking
- Integration with payment systems and messaging networks

---

### Scene 3: AI Transaction Monitoring
**Duration:** 3-4 minutes  
**Key Technologies:** Mosaic AI, Unity Catalog, ML Model Governance  
**Value Story:** Demonstrate how ML models detect suspicious behavioral patterns that traditional rule-based systems miss. Show model governance and explainability features that enable compliance teams to understand and trust AI-driven decisions.

**Key Talking Points:**
- Advanced pattern detection using ML models
- Model governance and versioning in Unity Catalog
- Explainable AI for regulatory compliance
- Continuous model monitoring and retraining

---

### Scene 4: Agentic Assisted Investigation
**Duration:** 4-5 minutes  
**Key Technologies:** Agent Bricks, Unstructured Data Processing, Vector Search  
**Value Story:** Show how an AI investigator uncovers complex relationships (e.g., Panama Papers links, sanctioned shadow fleet connections) in minutes instead of days. Demonstrate how AI agents analyze structured and unstructured data to build comprehensive investigation reports.

**Key Talking Points:**
- AI-powered investigation automation
- Analysis of unstructured data (documents, news, public records)
- Relationship mapping and network analysis
- Automated report generation with evidence trails

---

### Scene 5: SAR Filing & Case Closure
**Duration:** 3-4 minutes  
**Key Technologies:** Agent Bricks, Delta Sharing, Regulatory Reporting  
**Value Story:** Demonstrate how MLRO reviews AI-generated Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) with complete audit trail. Show one-click submission to regulatory authorities and seamless case closure workflows.

**Key Talking Points:**
- Automated SAR generation with regulatory compliance
- Complete audit trail for regulatory submissions
- Secure data sharing with regulatory authorities via Delta Sharing
- Case management and workflow automation

---

### Scene 6: CCO Executive Dashboard
**Duration:** 4-5 minutes  
**Key Technologies:** Databricks AI/BI Dashboards, Genie (Conversational AI)  
**Value Story:** Show real-time AML/KYC metrics dashboard with AI-powered Genie assistant for natural language Q&A. Demonstrate how executives can get instant insights into compliance KPIs, alert volumes, SAR conversion rates, and operational metrics.

**Key Talking Points:**
- Real-time executive dashboards with key compliance metrics
- Natural language querying via Genie for ad-hoc analysis
- Comprehensive KPI tracking (alert volumes, SAR filing rates, SLA compliance, etc.)
- Self-service analytics for compliance teams

## Demo Tips for Presenters

1. **Start with Business Value:** Always lead with business outcomes, not technology features
2. **Tell a Story:** Connect the scenes to show a complete customer journey
3. **Emphasize Integration:** Highlight how all components work together seamlessly
4. **Address Compliance:** Explicitly mention regulatory compliance and audit capabilities
5. **Show Real-Time:** Emphasize the real-time nature of the platform
6. **Demonstrate AI:** Show how AI enhances human decision-making, not replaces it
7. **Highlight Governance:** Stress the importance of data governance and explainability
