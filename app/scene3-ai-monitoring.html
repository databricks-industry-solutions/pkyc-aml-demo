Structuring Pattern - Detailed Analysis

Typology: Transaction Structuring (Smurfing)

Structuring, also known as smurfing, is the practice of breaking up large financial transactions into smaller amounts to avoid regulatory reporting requirements. In the European Union, cash transactions and wire transfers exceeding €10,000 trigger mandatory reporting to financial intelligence units (FIUs).
